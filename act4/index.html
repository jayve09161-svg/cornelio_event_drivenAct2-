❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to buy parts before you visit?
No. We bring testing gear and common spares. If you need a specific part, we can order it or send you a direct link.

Q: What if my PC can't be fixed at my desk?
Over 90% of repairs are done on-site. If micro-soldering or extensive bench testing is needed, we issue an intake receipt and repair it at our base with your permission.

Q: How much does a home visit cost?
We use upfront flat pricing. Your diagnostic fee goes directly toward the repair cost if you choose to proceed.

Q: Will my personal files stay safe?
Yes. Since repairs happen in front of you, your data never leaves your sight. We also back up files before major fixes.
